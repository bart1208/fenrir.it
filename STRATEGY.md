# fenrir.it — what this site is for

Rewritten 2026-09-03. The previous version of this document described a "Digital Declaration of
Independence" organised around three pillars — The Forge, The Observatory, The Nomad. That framing
produced a site where a reader could not find out who I am or what I build, so it has been replaced.
The earlier intent is kept below, because the reason it failed is the useful part.

## Purpose

One reader, two questions, in this order:

1. **Who is this and what has he actually built?** Answerable in about five seconds, above the fold,
   with a real name attached to it.
2. **Is there a mind here worth talking to?** Answerable by reading further, if they want to.

Question 2 is what makes the site worth having at all — a portfolio that only answers question 1 is
a worse LinkedIn. But question 1 has to be answered *first*, and unconditionally. That is the whole
correction.

## Principles

- **Name first, Fenrir as the mark.** The site used to present only "FENRIR". Someone searching for
  Bartolomeo Pasquale had no way to know they had found him. The wolf is the identity of the place;
  it is not a pseudonym to hide behind.
- **Nothing on the page that does not resolve.** Every project links to a live URL or a public repo,
  or is honestly labelled private and described rather than linked. The previous site listed four
  project names as decoration, one of which pointed at a service that had been retired.
- **Positioning comes from one source.** The wording here is derived from the professional-profile
  page in the private knowledge base, which is the source of truth. This site is a render target of
  it, never the reverse. When they disagree, this site is wrong.
- **The essay stays, demoted.** The argument about intent is genuinely mine and genuinely
  differentiating; it is also not the front door. It lives under Writing, below the work.
- **English only.** See README.
- **Prose is written.** The site previously accumulated several hundred words of generated metaphor
  about a stream carving a channel, across three languages, in a terminal widget. Anything that
  cannot state its own point in one sentence does not go on the page.

## What the old version got right

The refusal to be corporate, the insistence that hands-on building matters, and the decision to
have a point of view at all. Those survive. What did not survive was expressing them through
metaphor instead of through evidence.
